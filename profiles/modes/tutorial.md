# Mode: tutorial video

The source is a tutorial that shows and explains each step. Freedom: **low**. The end result must be the one in the video.

- Follow the video's steps, rewritten the way they work today. Most sections are `Source: video`.
- Classify the whole video into continuous SKIP / READ / WATCH stretches (intros, outros and long explanations are SKIP; menus, shortcuts and values are READ; things done by eye are WATCH; when in doubt, WATCH) and give the Video map.
- Use the contact sheets to confirm what is selected, which menu opens and how each step looks, instead of guessing from the transcript.
- If the transcript is machine-generated, add a folded callout at the top with misrecognised words and their meaning.
- Where the instructor is unclear, apply "If something is unclear" from the rules, and mark it.
